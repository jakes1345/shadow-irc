Connect

1Pick your client

Use the web client at app.shadowspace.space, or connect with any IRC client (HexChat, WeeChat, irssi) to:

shadowspace.space:6697 (TLS)
wss://app.shadowspace.space (WebSocket)

2Register your nick

No email needed. Once connected, register in-protocol:

/msg NickServ REGISTER <password>
/msg NickServ IDENTIFY <password>

3Join or create channels

Default channels are #cosmos and #quantum. Create your own anytime:

/join #yourchannel
/msg ChanServ REGISTER #yourchannel
